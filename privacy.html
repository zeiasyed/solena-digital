Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Solena Digital ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit solena-digital.com or contact us for services.

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, deliver our services, send relevant updates about your project, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

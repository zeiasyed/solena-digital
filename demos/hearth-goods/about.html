Our Story

From a farmers market table to a nationwide specialty retailer — built on craft, curation, and community.

Founded 2019

Gathering the makers we believe in

Hearth & Co. began when founders Elena and Marcus set up a single table at the Portland Farmers Market, selling curated gift boxes of local honey, olive oil, and preserves.

Customers kept asking for more — and for shipping to friends and family across the country. That demand shaped everything: a Shopify storefront, a warehouse partnership, and a sourcing philosophy that puts small-batch quality first.

Today we work with over 40 independent artisans, from Vermont maple producers to California olive growers, bringing their best work to tables nationwide.

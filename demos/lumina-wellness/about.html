Our Story

A sanctuary in the city

Lumina Wellness Studio was founded on a simple belief: urban life deserves a place to pause, breathe, and move with intention.

Hayes Valley, San Francisco

Built for the urban wellness seeker

Opened in 2019, Lumina occupies a sun-drenched corner of Hayes Valley — a neighborhood known for its creative energy and walkable charm. We designed every detail of our 2,400 sq ft studio to feel like a retreat: natural light, warm wood tones, and a palette that soothes before class even begins.

Our class sizes stay intentionally small. Whether you're flowing through vinyasa, working a reformer circuit, or settling into meditation, you'll receive personal attention from instructors who know your name and your practice.

We serve San Francisco's diverse urban market — tech professionals, creatives, parents, and newcomers alike — with classes that meet you where you are, not where you think you should be.

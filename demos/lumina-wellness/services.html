Class Types

What we offer

Flow Yoga

Dynamic vinyasa linking breath to movement. Options range from slow, restorative flows to athletic power sequences. Suitable for all levels with modifications offered throughout.

60 min Heated optional

Reformer Pilates

Low-impact, high-intensity movement on professional reformer equipment. Build core stability, improve alignment, and develop long, lean strength — ideal for desk-bound city professionals.

50 min Max 8 students

Mindful Meditation

Guided sessions incorporating breathwork, body scans, and mindfulness techniques. No experience needed — arrive as you are and leave feeling grounded and clear.

45 min Cushions provided

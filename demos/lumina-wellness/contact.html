First visit

What to expect

Arrive 10 minutes early for your first class. Our team will show you around, help you select props, and answer any questions about yoga, pilates, or meditation.

Wear comfortable, breathable clothing. We provide mats, blocks, straps, and reformer equipment. Hydration stations and changing areas are available on-site.

After your visit, you'll receive a welcome email with class recommendations, a link to follow us on Instagram, and an invitation to join our weekly nurture newsletter.

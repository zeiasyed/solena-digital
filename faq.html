Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from business owners considering a new website or marketing program.

How much does a new website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, features, and integrations you need. Most small business websites range from $2,500–$8,000 for design and development. We'll provide a detailed quote after understanding your goals — there's no charge for the initial consultation.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical small business website takes 4–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger sites or e-commerce projects may take 8–12 weeks. We provide a timeline during the proposal phase and keep you updated throughout.

Do you work with businesses outside my area?

Yes. We serve clients nationwide. All communication happens via email, phone, and video call — location doesn't limit the quality of our work.

What's included in your monthly SEO service?

Our SEO programs include keyword research, on-page optimization, technical audits, content recommendations, local citation building, Google Business Profile management, and a monthly performance report with rankings, traffic, and next-step recommendations.

Do I own my website?

Absolutely. You own all content, design assets, and domain. If you ever decide to leave, we'll provide a full export of your site files. We don't hold websites hostage.

What kind of reports will I receive?

Every active client receives a monthly report covering organic traffic, keyword rankings, leads generated, and conversion metrics. Reports include both an interactive dashboard summary and a detailed PDF you can share with your team. See samples here.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We regularly take outdated sites and rebuild them with modern design, mobile optimization, improved SEO, and faster load times — often while preserving your existing search rankings.

Do you require long-term contracts?

Website projects are one-time engagements. Ongoing services like SEO and hosting are month-to-month after an initial 3-month commitment to allow time for results. No hidden cancellation fees.

How do I get started?

Fill out our contact form or email us at info@solena-digital.com. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your business, goals, and budget.

Still have questions?

We're happy to answer anything not covered here. Reach out and we'll get back to you within one business day.
